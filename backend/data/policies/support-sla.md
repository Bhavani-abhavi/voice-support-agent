# Support Channels and Response Times

## Response times by plan

Free customers get help from the community forum and email support, with a first response within 3 business days. Pro customers get email and chat support with a first response within 24 hours. Business customers get priority support with a first response within 4 hours, and phone support for urgent issues.

## Escalation to a human

The AI agent hands a conversation to a human specialist when it cannot find the answer in the knowledge base with enough confidence, when a request is outside its limits (such as refunds above its approval limit or contract changes), when a tool or system it needs is unavailable, or whenever the customer asks for a person. The escalation ticket includes a summary so the customer does not have to repeat themselves.

## Ticket priorities

Urgent: the customer cannot access the account or there is a security concern. High: billing errors, refunds above the agent limit and Business contract requests. Normal: everything else.
