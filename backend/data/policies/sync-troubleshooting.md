# Sync and App Troubleshooting

## Notes not syncing

First, check the status page at status.orbitnotes.example for an ongoing incident. If there is no incident, ask the customer to make sure they are on the latest app version, then sign out and sign back in. On mobile, they should also check that background app refresh is allowed for Orbit Notes.

If notes still do not sync after signing back in, the customer can use Settings, then Advanced, then Rebuild local cache. This does not delete any notes that are already synced.

## Device limits

The Free plan syncs on up to 2 devices. If a Free customer adds a third device, sync pauses on the new device until they remove an old one or upgrade to Pro. Pro and Business have no device limit.

## Conflict copies

If the same note is edited on two devices while offline, Orbit Notes keeps both versions and names one "conflict copy". Nothing is lost; the customer can merge the two notes and delete the copy.

## Offline mode
<!-- tiers: pro, business -->

Pro and Business customers can mark notebooks as available offline in the notebook menu. Offline notebooks are stored on the device and sync automatically when the connection returns.
