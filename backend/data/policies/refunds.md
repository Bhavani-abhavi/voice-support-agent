# Refunds and Billing Disputes

## Duplicate or accidental charges

If a customer was charged twice for the same billing period, the duplicate charge is always refunded in full, on any paid plan. Duplicate charges can be refunded up to 90 days after the charge date. Support agents may issue a duplicate-charge refund without approval once the customer's identity is verified.

Refunds go back to the original payment method and appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on the customer's bank.

## Pro monthly plans
<!-- tiers: pro -->

Pro monthly subscriptions can be refunded in full within 14 days of the charge date. After 14 days, the charge is not refundable, but the customer can cancel at any time to stop the next renewal.

Support agents may approve a Pro refund of up to $150 per request. Larger amounts need a billing specialist.

## Pro annual plans
<!-- tiers: pro -->

Pro annual subscriptions can be refunded in full within 30 days of the charge date. After 30 days, annual plans are not refundable, but auto-renew can be turned off so the plan does not renew next year.

## Business plans
<!-- tiers: business -->

Business plans are billed under a signed contract. Support agents may issue refunds or account credits of up to $500 per request for billing errors. Any refund above $500, and any request to end a contract early, must be handled by the customer's account manager, so the agent creates an escalation ticket with high priority.

## Free plan
<!-- tiers: free -->

The Free plan has no charges, so there is nothing to refund. If a Free customer sees a charge from Orbit Notes, treat it as a possible billing error and escalate it.

## What agents must not do

Agents never promise a refund before checking the invoice and this policy. Agents never ask for full card numbers, bank details or passwords.
