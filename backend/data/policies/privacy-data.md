# Privacy and Data Requests

## Deleting personal data

Customers can ask for all of their personal data to be deleted. The request is confirmed by email, and deletion is completed within 30 days. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone, so customers should export their notes first.

## Data location
<!-- tiers: business -->

Business workspaces can choose to store data in the United States or the European Union. Changing the data region for an existing workspace is handled by the account manager.

## Who can see notes

Orbit Notes staff cannot read note contents, except when the customer gives written permission for a specific support investigation. Support agents can see account details such as plan, invoices and device list, but not notes.
