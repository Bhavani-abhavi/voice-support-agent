# Cancellation and Account Closure

## Cancelling a Pro subscription
<!-- tiers: pro -->

Pro customers can cancel at any time from Settings, then Billing, then Cancel plan. The plan stays active until the end of the period that was already paid for, and then the account returns to the Free plan. Cancelling does not trigger a refund on its own; refunds follow the refund policy.

## Ending a Business contract
<!-- tiers: business -->

Business contracts run for the full contract term. Early termination, seat reductions below the contracted minimum and contract changes are handled only by the customer's account manager. Support agents create an escalation ticket for these requests and do not promise any outcome.

## What happens to data after cancellation

After a paid plan ends, the account keeps all notes on the Free plan limits. Notebooks over the Free limit become read-only, not deleted. If a customer closes their account completely, their data is kept for 30 days in case they change their mind, and is then permanently deleted.

## Exporting data

Customers can export all notes at any time from Settings, then Data, then Export. Exports are available as Markdown or PDF and are emailed as a download link that expires after 7 days.
