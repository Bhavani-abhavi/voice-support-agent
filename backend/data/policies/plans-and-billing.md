# Plans, Pricing and Billing

## Plans and prices

Orbit Notes has three plans. Free costs $0 and includes 3 notebooks and sync on 2 devices. Pro costs $12 per month or $120 per year and includes unlimited notebooks, sync on unlimited devices, offline mode and version history. Business costs $25 per user per month, billed annually under a contract, and adds SSO, admin controls, audit logs and priority support.

## Billing dates and invoices

Paid plans renew on the same day each month or year as the original purchase. Invoices are emailed to the billing email on file and are also available in Settings, then Billing, then Invoices. Customers can change their billing email from the same page, or support can change it after verifying identity.

## Failed payments and past-due accounts

If a renewal payment fails, the account becomes past due. Orbit Notes retries the card after 1, 3 and 7 days. During this 7-day grace period the customer keeps full access. After 7 days without a successful payment, a Pro account moves to read-only mode until the balance is paid. Notes are never deleted because of a failed payment.

## Changing plans

Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades from Pro to Free take effect at the end of the current billing period. Business seat changes are handled by the workspace admin and billed on the next invoice.
